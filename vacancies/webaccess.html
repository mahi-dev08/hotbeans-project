Web Accessibility Designer

Accessibility UX Inclusive Design

Skills Required:

  • Strong understanding of WCAG guidelines and accessibility standards
  • Ability to evaluate websites and digital products for accessibility issues
  • Experience using accessibility testing tools such as WAVE, Axe, or Lighthouse
  • Knowledge of inclusive design principles and assistive technologies

Overview:

The Web Accessibility Designer will ensure that all digital experiences are usable and accessible for people of all abilities. You will work closely with designers, developers, and product teams to identify accessibility barriers and recommend improvements. This role requires a strong understanding of inclusive design and a passion for creating digital products that everyone can use.

This position is ideal for someone who is detail‑oriented, empathetic, and committed to improving accessibility across the web.

Technical Skills:

  • Knowledge of WCAG 2.1/2.2 and accessibility best practices
  • Experience with screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS, or VoiceOver
  • Ability to run accessibility audits and document findings
  • Understanding of semantic HTML, ARIA roles, and keyboard navigation
  • Ability to collaborate with developers to implement accessible solutions

Qualifications:

  • Experience in UX, UI, or accessibility‑focused design roles
  • Strong understanding of inclusive design and user needs
  • Excellent communication and documentation skills
  • Ability to work collaboratively with cross‑functional teams
