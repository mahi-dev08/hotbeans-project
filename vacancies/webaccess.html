Web Accessibility Designer

Accessibility UX Inclusive Design

Who we need:

  • A passionate advocate with strong expertise in WCAG guidelines and accessibility standards
  • Someone skilled at evaluating websites and digital products for accessibility issues
  • An experienced tester comfortable using accessibility tools like WAVE, Axe, and Lighthouse
  • A thoughtful designer who understands inclusive design principles and assistive technologies

Overview:

We care about making our products work for everyone, regardless of their abilities. You'll evaluate our designs and code for accessibility issues, recommend improvements, and help build genuinely inclusive experiences. It requires both technical knowledge and real empathy for the users who depend on accessible design.

If you believe accessibility is a core feature and you're motivated by making the web genuinely inclusive, this role is for you.

Technical Skills:

  • Knowledge of WCAG 2.1/2.2 and accessibility best practices
  • Experience with screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS, or VoiceOver
  • Ability to run accessibility audits and document findings
  • Understanding of semantic HTML, ARIA roles, and keyboard navigation
  • Ability to collaborate with developers to implement accessible solutions

Qualifications:

  • Experience in UX, UI, or accessibility‑focused design roles
  • Strong understanding of inclusive design and user needs
  • Excellent communication and documentation skills
  • Ability to work collaboratively with cross‑functional teams
