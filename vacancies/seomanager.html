SEO Manager

SEO Marketing Strategy

Who we need:

  • A strategic thinker with deep expertise in SEO strategy, analytics, and performance optimization
  • An inspiring leader capable of driving SEO campaigns and managing a talented team
  • A data-driven professional experienced with tools like Google Analytics, Search Console, SEMrush, and Ahrefs
  • A comprehensive SEO expert with knowledge of technical SEO, content optimization, and link-building

Overview:

We want someone who can lead our SEO efforts and grow our organic presence strategically. You'll build and execute SEO strategies, lead a team, and collaborate with content and development teams to improve how we rank in search results. It's equal parts data analysis, creative strategy, and staying on top of how search engines evolve.

If you're energized by growth metrics and love the challenge of competitive SEO landscapes, this is the role for you.

Technical Skills:

  • Proficiency with SEO tools (SEMrush, Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Google Analytics)
  • Strong understanding of technical SEO, Core Web Vitals, and site performance
  • Ability to conduct audits, keyword research, and competitor analysis
  • Experience with structured data, sitemaps, and indexation management
  • Understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from an SEO perspective

Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in a senior SEO or SEO management role
  • Strong leadership and communication skills
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and guide a team effectively
  • Proven track record of improving organic performance and search rankings
