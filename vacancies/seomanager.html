SEO Manager

SEO Marketing Strategy

Skills Required:

  • Strong understanding of SEO strategy, analytics, and performance optimisation
  • Ability to lead SEO campaigns and manage a team of specialists
  • Experience with tools such as Google Analytics, Search Console, SEMrush, and Ahrefs
  • Knowledge of technical SEO, content optimisation, and link‑building strategies

Overview:

The SEO Manager will oversee the planning, execution, and optimisation of our SEO strategy. You will lead a team of SEO specialists, collaborate with content creators and developers, and ensure our digital presence continues to grow organically. This role involves analysing performance data, identifying opportunities, and implementing strategies that improve search visibility and user engagement.

This position is ideal for someone who is strategic, data‑driven, and passionate about staying ahead of search engine trends and algorithm updates.

Technical Skills:

  • Proficiency with SEO tools (SEMrush, Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Google Analytics)
  • Strong understanding of technical SEO, Core Web Vitals, and site performance
  • Ability to conduct audits, keyword research, and competitor analysis
  • Experience with structured data, sitemaps, and indexation management
  • Understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from an SEO perspective

Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in a senior SEO or SEO management role
  • Strong leadership and communication skills
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and guide a team effectively
  • Proven track record of improving organic performance and search rankings
