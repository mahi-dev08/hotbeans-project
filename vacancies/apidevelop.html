Network Lead

Network Infrastructure Leadership

Who we need:

  • An experienced infrastructure professional with expertise in designing and managing web services and cloud platforms
  • Someone with proven leadership skills and experience managing technical teams
  • A DevOps architect comfortable with load balancing, CDNs, containerization, and web security
  • A strategic thinker who can optimize application performance and ensure system reliability at scale

Overview:

We need someone to lead our web infrastructure and keep everything running reliably and securely. You'll manage our cloud platforms, oversee CDN and containerization strategies, and lead a team of infrastructure engineers. It's a mix of strategic planning, technical depth, and hands-on problem-solving at scale.

If you enjoy leading teams, optimizing complex systems, and staying current with DevOps innovations, this is the place to apply that expertise.

Technical Skills:

  • Deep expertise in cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and their networking services
  • Proficiency with load balancing, reverse proxies, and CDN management (Cloudflare, Akamai, etc.)
  • Knowledge of containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes) and infrastructure-as-code
  • Experience with web security, SSL/TLS, DDoS protection, and application firewalls
  • Familiarity with monitoring and observability tools for web applications and services

Qualifications:

  • Minimum 6+ years of web infrastructure or DevOps experience with at least 2 years in a leadership role
  • Cloud certifications such as AWS Solutions Architect, Azure Administrator, or Kubernetes certifications (highly preferred)
  • Proven track record of managing high-traffic web applications and cloud infrastructure
  • Strong leadership, communication, and project management skills
