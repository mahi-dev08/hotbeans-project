UX Lead Designer

UX Design Leadership

Skills Required:

  • Strong understanding of user experience principles and design systems
  • Ability to lead design workshops and guide junior designers
  • Proficiency in tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, or Sketch
  • Experience conducting user research and usability testing

Overview:

The UX Lead Designer will be responsible for shaping intuitive, user‑focused digital experiences across our products. You will oversee the full design process — from research and wireframing to prototyping and final UI delivery. This role requires strong leadership, creativity, and the ability to collaborate closely with product managers, developers, and stakeholders.

This position is ideal for someone who is passionate about solving user problems, improving product usability, and mentoring designers to deliver high‑quality work.

Technical Skills:

  • Proficiency with Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or similar tools
  • Strong understanding of UX research methods and usability testing
  • Ability to create wireframes, prototypes, and high‑fidelity UI designs
  • Knowledge of accessibility standards and responsive design
  • Understanding of basic HTML/CSS to communicate effectively with developers

Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in a senior or lead UX/UI design role
  • Strong portfolio showcasing user‑centered design work
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and lead design direction
