UX Junior Designer

UX Design Entry Level

Skills Required:

  • Basic understanding of UX principles and user‑centered design
  • Ability to create simple wireframes and prototypes
  • Familiarity with design tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, or Sketch
  • Willingness to learn, take feedback, and grow within the team

Overview:

The UX Junior Designer will support the design team in creating user‑friendly digital experiences. You will assist with research, wireframing, prototyping, and preparing design assets for development. This role is perfect for someone starting their UX career who is eager to learn, collaborate, and build strong design foundations.

You will work closely with senior designers and product teams to understand user needs and contribute to improving the overall usability of our products.

Technical Skills:

  • Basic proficiency with Figma, Adobe XD, or similar tools
  • Understanding of wireframing and prototyping techniques
  • Ability to participate in user research and note‑taking
  • Knowledge of responsive design and accessibility basics
  • Good communication skills for presenting ideas and receiving feedback

Qualifications:

  • Some experience with UX/UI design (projects, coursework, or internships)
  • A portfolio showcasing design work or university projects
  • Strong willingness to learn and develop new skills
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment
