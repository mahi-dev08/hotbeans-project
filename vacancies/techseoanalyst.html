Technical SEO Analyst

SEO Technical Analytics

Who we need

  • A technically-minded professional with strong expertise in technical SEO principles and website architecture
  • Someone skilled at conducting thorough site audits and identifying crawl, indexation, and performance issues
  • A data analyst experienced with tools like Google Search Console, Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, and SEMrush
  • An expert in Core Web Vitals, structured data, and mobile optimization

Overview:

The Technical SEO Analyst will be responsible for analysing, diagnosing, and improving the technical performance of our websites. You will work closely with developers, content teams, and marketing specialists to ensure our digital platforms follow SEO best practices and meet search engine requirements. This role involves conducting audits, monitoring site health, and implementing technical improvements that enhance visibility and organic performance.

This position is ideal for someone who is analytical, detail‑oriented, and passionate about solving complex SEO challenges.

Technical Skills:

  • Proficiency with SEO auditing tools (Screaming Frog, Sitebulb, Ahrefs, SEMrush)
  • Understanding of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and how they impact SEO
  • Experience with structured data (Schema.org) and rich results
  • Knowledge of site speed optimisation and Core Web Vitals
  • Ability to analyse log files, sitemaps, and robots.txt configurations

Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in a technical SEO or SEO analyst role
  • Strong analytical and problem‑solving skills
  • Ability to interpret data and present insights clearly
  • Comfort working with developers and cross‑functional teams
