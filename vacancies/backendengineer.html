Backend Engineer

Engineering Backend APIs

Who we need:

  • An experienced backend developer with strong expertise in at least one major backend language
  • Someone who can architect and build reliable, scalable APIs
  • A developer comfortable working with both relational and NoSQL databases
  • A thoughtful engineer who prioritizes security, performance, and code quality

Overview:

We need someone who can build the foundation that keeps our applications running smoothly. You'll own the server-side logic that powers our products and work closely with frontend engineers and product teams to deliver features that actually work. It's a mix of architecture decisions, problem-solving, and making sure everything stays performant and secure.

If you enjoy writing clean code and taking on challenging technical problems, this role offers plenty of that.

Technical Skills:

  • Proficiency in a backend language or framework (e.g. Node.js/Express, Django, Spring, .NET)
  • Experience with SQL and/or NoSQL databases (e.g. PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB)
  • Knowledge of API design, authentication, and authorization
  • Familiarity with version control (Git) and CI/CD workflows
  • Understanding of cloud platforms or containerisation (e.g. AWS, Azure, Docker)

Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in a backend or full‑stack engineering role
  • Strong problem‑solving and debugging skills
  • Ability to write clean, well‑documented, and testable code
  • Comfort working in a collaborative, agile team environment
