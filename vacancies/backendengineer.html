Backend Engineer

Engineering Backend APIs

Skills Required:

  • Strong experience with at least one backend language (e.g. Node.js, Java, Python, C#)
  • Ability to design, build, and maintain RESTful or GraphQL APIs
  • Experience working with relational and/or NoSQL databases
  • Understanding of security, performance, and scalability best practices

Overview:

The Backend Engineer will be responsible for building and maintaining the server‑side logic that powers our applications. You will work closely with frontend engineers, product managers, and designers to deliver reliable, secure, and scalable features. This role involves designing APIs, managing data storage, and ensuring smooth integration between services.

This position is ideal for someone who enjoys solving complex technical problems, writing clean and maintainable code, and improving system performance.

Technical Skills:

  • Proficiency in a backend language or framework (e.g. Node.js/Express, Django, Spring, .NET)
  • Experience with SQL and/or NoSQL databases (e.g. PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB)
  • Knowledge of API design, authentication, and authorization
  • Familiarity with version control (Git) and CI/CD workflows
  • Understanding of cloud platforms or containerisation (e.g. AWS, Azure, Docker)

Qualifications:

  • Previous experience in a backend or full‑stack engineering role
  • Strong problem‑solving and debugging skills
  • Ability to write clean, well‑documented, and testable code
  • Comfort working in a collaborative, agile team environment
